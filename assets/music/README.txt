Add your background music file here as invite.mp3
(or update musicUrl in data/config.js to point to your file).

Recommended: a short instrumental loop (30–90 sec) exported from Canva or your own royalty-free track.

Mobile browsers (iPhone/Android) play MP3 much more reliably than YouTube background music.
If music works on laptop but not on your phone, add invite.mp3 and set in data/config.js:

  musicUrl: "assets/music/invite.mp3",
  youtubeMusicId: "",
